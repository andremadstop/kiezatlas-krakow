Buildings from BDOT10k

The basis is the building layer of the BDOT10k database (latest object version: [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stand_daten|datum]]). Buildings in the city: [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stadt.anzahl]]; residential: [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stadt.wohngebaeude]], of which single-family [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stadt.wohn_art.ein]] and multi-family [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stadt.wohn_art.mehr]]. Median number of storeys of residential buildings: [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stadt.geschosse_median_wohn]]. Buildings cover [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:stadt.bebauungsdichte.wert|anteil]] of the city's area.

Each building belongs to exactly one area: a point inside the footprint decides. Check: [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:kontrolle.in_stadt]] of [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:kontrolle.datensaetze]] records lie in the city, each in one delegatura. The approximate floor area (footprint × storeys) is only a guide value, because BDOT10k gives no usable floor area.

The “3D buildings” button on the map switches on the 3D view (at close zoom, only for the districts in view). Building heights come from the LoD2 models; without a model, storeys × [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:methode.geschoss_hoehe_m]] m, marked as an estimate. Colour and the pop-up show the origin of the height for every building. Source notice for the heights: [[gebaeude_kennzahlen:quellen.hoehen.vermerk|text]]

What the atlas calls a kamienica

In the atlas a kamienica (tenement house) is a multi-family building according to BDOT10k with a link to a monument: a match in the register of monuments, or an OpenStreetMap object with a heritage tag inside the footprint or right next to it. A building named “Kamienica …” in BDOT10k counts separately as a link by name (such buildings: [[denkmale_bdot:zaehlung.namensbezug]]). Multi-family buildings in BDOT10k: [[denkmale_bdot:zaehlung.mehrfamilienhaeuser]].

This is always a subset: the atlas does not claim how many kamienice a district has, and it shows no construction years for areas. The year of construction in the register is paid for, and the population grid does not contain it. A construction period appears only in classes, never as a single year, and only where a source gives it.

Atlas of the ZBK stock

The source is the “Wykaz budynków i obiektów w zarządzie ZBK” (list of buildings managed by the municipal building authority) as of [[sanierung:stand|datum]], published as a PDF in Kraków's Biuletyn Informacji Publicznej. Entries: [[sanierung:liste.eintraege]], of which outside Kraków (Wieliczka): [[sanierung:liste.miejscowosc.wieliczka]]. Ownership according to the list: municipality [[sanierung:liste.je_eigentum.gmina]], State Treasury [[sanierung:liste.je_eigentum.skarb_panstwa]], mixed public [[sanierung:liste.je_eigentum.publiczne_mieszane]], with private shares [[sanierung:liste.je_eigentum.z_prywatnymi]], private [[sanierung:liste.je_eigentum.prywatne]]. We reuse the information under the conditions of the Gmina Miejska Kraków; the notice and the liability clause are in the data and in the downloads.

Data protection. The files contain no addresses, entry numbers or building identifiers. Only buildings wholly in public ownership (municipality, State Treasury, mixed public), without independent possession and without single-family houses, get a point on the map; there are [[sanierung_odbl:punkte.features|anzahl]] such points. All other entries are only counted by district, because a point at a house with a private share would link an address to a particular person's property.

Location. The addresses from the list were looked up among OpenStreetMap addresses: [[sanierung_odbl:verortung.je_klasse.verortet]] of [[sanierung_odbl:verortung.in_krakow]] entries in Kraków were found, that is [[sanierung_odbl:verortung.quote|anteil]] (Wilson 95 % interval: [[sanierung_odbl:verortung.wilson95.0|anteil]] to [[sanierung_odbl:verortung.wilson95.1|anteil]]). Check: the district in the list matches the district of the point in [[sanierung_odbl:gegenprobe_dzielnica.gleich]] of [[sanierung_odbl:gegenprobe_dzielnica.n]] cases. Because the location comes from OpenStreetMap, all points are in the _odbl files.

Block typology (estimate)

Candidates for multi-family blocks are selected by a rule fixed before the calculation, from BDOT10k data only: a multi-family building in use; at least [[sanierung:typologie.kriterien.geschosse_min]] storeys; a footprint of at least [[sanierung:typologie.kriterien.flaeche_min_m2]] m²; rectangularity of at least [[sanierung:typologie.kriterien.rechteckigkeit_min|zahl2]]. An estate (osiedle) is a block with at least [[sanierung:typologie.kriterien.nachbarn_min]] such blocks within [[sanierung:typologie.kriterien.umkreis_m]] m. Across the city: multi-family buildings [[sanierung:typologie.stadt.mfh]], block candidates [[sanierung:typologie.stadt.blok]], of which on estates [[sanierung:typologie.stadt.osiedle]].

This is an estimate, not a register of prefabricated buildings: it contains neither construction year nor energy use[[wenn sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung]]. A random sample has been checked on the orthophoto (Ortofotomapa, [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.datum|datum]]), only for whether a residential block is visible, not how it was built: of [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.n]] candidates in large estates yes [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.ja]], no [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.nein]], unclear [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.unklar]], i.e. [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.quote|anteil0]] of the decided cases (Wilson 95% interval [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.wilson95.0|anteil0]]–[[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.osiedle.wilson95.1|anteil0]]); as a control, [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.kein_kandidat.n]] multi-family buildings with at least [[sanierung:typologie.kriterien.geschosse_min]] storeys that are not candidates: yes [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.kein_kandidat.ja]], no [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.kein_kandidat.nein]], unclear [[sanierung:typologie.sichtpruefung.kein_kandidat.unklar]].[[sonst]], and the sample for visual checking has not been assessed yet.[[ende]] Literature cited bibliographically only: Barnaś, Jeleński, Nowak-Ocłoń, Racoń-Leja et al., [[sanierung:typologie.literatur.zeitschrift|text]], DOI [[sanierung:typologie.literatur.doi|text]]. The atlas has not checked the content of this article and uses no figures or data from it.
