      Limitations

      • Built-up land also includes yards and gardens within built-up areas, and squares include unpaved ones. This is an approximation, not a measurement of sealed surfaces.
      • Arable land is not counted as green space: it is not generally accessible and lies bare for part of the year.
      • Land surface temperature (heat) is not shown: no open source with a clear licence has been checked for this edition.
      • The accuracy of the BDOT10k land cover has not been checked against orthophotos or in the field.
      • Green space per resident is not calculated for grid squares, only for districts and the whole city.
      • District boundaries come from OpenStreetMap; district values are therefore published under the ODbL.
