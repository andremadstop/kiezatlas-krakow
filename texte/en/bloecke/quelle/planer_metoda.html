Units and residents

The planner works on the cells of Eurostat's population grid (1 km); residents within the city: [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.ew]]. The residents of a cell are placed at its centre; this is a simplification, because residents can be spread unevenly within a cell. District values come from the cells whose centre lies in the district (boundaries from OpenStreetMap); resident numbers are rounded to [[planer_odbl:rundung.ew]]. All indicators are guide values.

Facilities

Schools, kindergartens, nurseries, hospitals and parks come from the BDOT10k database as complexes with their own area (here a point inside the area). Within the city: schools [[einrichtungen:features[properties.art=szkola,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], kindergartens [[einrichtungen:features[properties.art=przedszkole,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], nurseries [[einrichtungen:features[properties.art=zlobek,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], hospitals [[einrichtungen:features[properties.art=szpital,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], parks [[einrichtungen:features[properties.art=park,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]]. BDOT10k does not distinguish school types and gives neither places nor pupil numbers; kindergartens and nurseries in buildings without their own plot do not appear at all. Values for nurseries and kindergartens therefore do not reflect the real network. The RSPO school register is not included yet because its licence has not been checked.

Doctors and pharmacies come from OpenStreetMap (doctors: [[einrichtungen_odbl:features[properties.art=arzt]|anzahl]], pharmacies: [[einrichtungen_odbl:features[properties.art=apotheke]|anzahl]]), without names; their completeness has not been checked. Facilities just outside the city boundary count towards the distances, because from the outskirts they are often the nearest.

Straight line and walking route

For each cell the atlas gives the distance in metres from its centre to the nearest facility of each kind: as a straight line (files without OpenStreetMap) and as a walking route along the OpenStreetMap path network (_odbl files). The walking route is always longer and closer to everyday experience; the questions on the pages always show it where it is available.

School and kindergarten within reach

The Polish education act (Prawo oświatowe) sets the longest way for a child to school and kindergarten: [[richtwerte:bildung.szkola_podstawowa_droga.wortlaut|text]] ([[richtwerte:bildung.szkola_podstawowa_droga.seite|text]]). For kindergartens: [[richtwerte:bildung.przedszkole_droga.seite|text]], [[richtwerte:bildung.przedszkole_droga.wert_m]] m. Source: [[planer_odbl:quellen.prawo_oswiatowe.vermerk|text]].

The act means the way to the school of the catchment area where the child lives; the atlas computes the way to the nearest facility of any kind. This is therefore a guide value, not an official standard. Result for the whole city (walking route):

  • school within [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=szkola,richtwert.wert=3000].richtwert.wert]] m: [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=szkola,richtwert.wert=3000].wert|anteil0]] of residents ([[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=szkola,richtwert.wert=3000].zaehler]] of [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=szkola,richtwert.wert=3000].nenner]]);
  • kindergarten within [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=przedszkole].richtwert.wert]] m: [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=przedszkole].wert|anteil0]] ([[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=przedszkole].zaehler]] of [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=anteil_in_radius,art=przedszkole].nenner]]).

Service frequency class

Stops and departures come from the ZTP GTFS timetable. A stop is the common stem of the platform codes; its location is the mean of the platform locations. We count departures on the working day [[haltestellen:stichtag|datum]] between [[haltestellen:fenster.0|text]] and [[haltestellen:fenster.1|text]], all lines together, in the busier direction, as an hourly mean. Minimum departures per hour: class I [[haltestellen:klassen.grenzen.I]], class II [[haltestellen:klassen.grenzen.II]], class III [[haltestellen:klassen.grenzen.III]]. The thresholds were taken over from the Potsdam atlas so that both cities are comparable; no Polish standard was found for them, so the classes are guide values.

Stops within the city: [[haltestellen:features[properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], of which class I: [[haltestellen:features[properties.taktklasse=I,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], class II: [[haltestellen:features[properties.taktklasse=II,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], class III: [[haltestellen:features[properties.taktklasse=III,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]], below class III: [[haltestellen:features[properties.taktklasse=unter III,properties.in_stadt=true]|anzahl]]. Stops up to [[richtwerte:oepnv.messung.randpuffer_m]] m beyond the city boundary also count ([[haltestellen:features[properties.in_stadt=false]|anzahl]]).

A stop of class I or II within [[richtwerte:planer.oepnv_radius_m.wert]] m (our own choice, no standard) is available to [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=oepnv_takt].wert|anteil0]] of residents on foot and [[planer_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=oepnv_takt].wert|anteil0]] in a straight line.

Green space nearby

Parks are BDOT10k complexes “park lub skwer”, without forests and allotment gardens. Within walking reach (the radius is the default of the calculation script, no standard; verification records: belege/README.md) a park is available to [[planer_fuss_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=gruen_in_radius].wert|anteil0]] of residents; park area per resident across the city is [[planer_odbl:gesamt.werte[kennzahl=gruen_m2_je_ew].wert|zahl1]] m².

Limitations

  • The distance to the nearest facility says nothing about places, school catchment areas or opening hours.
  • A 1 km cell with its residents at the centre over- or underestimates the way for those living at its edge.
  • Frequency classes refer to one working day and the morning peak.
  • The walking network and doctors from OpenStreetMap are as complete as the map on the day of download.
