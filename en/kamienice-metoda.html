Tenements: method

How the tenement and monument layer is built: sources, matching, limitations.

Buildings from BDOT10k

The basis is the building layer of the BDOT10k database (latest object version: 18 May 2026). Buildings in the city: 90,563; residential: 61,199, of which single-family 46,817 and multi-family 13,739. Median number of storeys of residential buildings: 2. Buildings cover 7.1% of the city's area.

Each building belongs to exactly one area: a point inside the footprint decides. Check: 90,563 of 90,563 records lie in the city, each in one delegatura. The approximate floor area (footprint × storeys) is only a guide value, because BDOT10k gives no usable floor area.

The “3D buildings” button on the map switches on the 3D view (at close zoom, only for the districts in view). Building heights come from the LoD2 models; without a model, storeys × 3 m, marked as an estimate. Colour and the pop-up show the origin of the height for every building. Source notice for the heights: Wysokości: Modele 3D budynków (LoD2, stan 2017), Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii. „Modele 3D budynków są dostępne bezpłatnie i możliwe do dowolnego wykorzystania.” (https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/inne-dane/modele-3d-budynkow/)

What the atlas calls a kamienica

In the atlas a kamienica (tenement house) is a multi-family building according to BDOT10k with a link to a monument: a match in the register of monuments, or an OpenStreetMap object with a heritage tag inside the footprint or right next to it. A building named “Kamienica …” in BDOT10k counts separately as a link by name (such buildings: 28). Multi-family buildings in BDOT10k: 13,739.

This is always a subset: the atlas does not claim how many kamienice a district has, and it shows no construction years for areas. The year of construction in the register is paid for, and the population grid does not contain it. A construction period appears only in classes, never as a single year, and only where a source gives it.

Monuments: register and OpenStreetMap

The NID register of monuments and the gminna ewidencja zabytków (municipal heritage record) need a manual download that has not happened yet. Of the register, therefore, only the part carried by Wikidata is available (as of 14 November 2026): objects with a number of the Polish heritage register located in Kraków. 1,909 objects were read. Objects with the same number form one point at the coordinates of the first object (684 merged), 9 have no coordinates, 2 lie beyond the city boundary. This leaves 1,214 points in the city, 1,125 of them matched to a BDOT10k building and 571 in a multi-family building. The location comes from the coordinates in Wikidata and was checked on the orthophoto for a random sample (n = 20, 15 November 2026): on the object 17, just beside it 3, away from it 0, i.e. 85% on the object (Wilson 95% interval 64%–95%); the object on the orthophoto matches the description in 18 of 18 decided cases (unclear: 2). The result describes the layer, not every single point. This page gives the figures by delegatura; the free map shows the points as a layer of their own, separate from OpenStreetMap. Wikidata is not fetched automatically, because the robots.txt of the query service forbids it for programs; the data comes from a manual download.

From the OpenStreetMap extract (as of 12 November 2026) the atlas takes objects tagged heritage or start_date: in total 929, with heritage 184, with start_date 822, with a readable construction period 767. Matches with a BDOT10k building: 441, of which multi-family buildings 102. The location of the objects tagged heritage was checked on the orthophoto for a random sample (n = 20, 15 November 2026): on the object 16, just beside it 3, away from it 1, i.e. 80% on the object (Wilson 95% interval 58%–92%); the object on the orthophoto matches the description in 17 of 17 decided cases (unclear: 3); both layers together: 33 of 40 on the object, 83%. The result describes the layer, not every single point; the rule for the assessment was fixed before counting (“just beside” does not count as “on the object”). The assignment to a building has not been checked.

BDOT10k complexes (forts, building ensembles, museums, memorial sites) are shown as topography, not as registered monuments: forts 38, building ensembles 17, museums 3, memorial sites 1.

Atlas of the ZBK stock

The source is the “Wykaz budynków i obiektów w zarządzie ZBK” (list of buildings managed by the municipal building authority) as of 1 December 2025, published as a PDF in Kraków's Biuletyn Informacji Publicznej. Entries: 508, of which outside Kraków (Wieliczka): 8. Ownership according to the list: municipality 410, State Treasury 34, mixed public 4, with private shares 46, private 14. We reuse the information under the conditions of the Gmina Miejska Kraków; the notice and the liability clause are in the data and in the downloads.

Data protection. The files contain no addresses, entry numbers or building identifiers. Only buildings wholly in public ownership (municipality, State Treasury, mixed public), without independent possession and without single-family houses, get a point on the map; there are 126 such points. All other entries are only counted by district, because a point at a house with a private share would link an address to a particular person's property.

Location. The addresses from the list were looked up among OpenStreetMap addresses: 398 of 500 entries in Kraków were found, that is 79.6% (Wilson 95 % interval: 75.8% to 82.9%). Check: the district in the list matches the district of the point in 398 of 406 cases. Because the location comes from OpenStreetMap, all points are in the _odbl files.

Block typology (estimate)

Candidates for multi-family blocks are selected by a rule fixed before the calculation, from BDOT10k data only: a multi-family building in use; at least 4 storeys; a footprint of at least 250 m²; rectangularity of at least 0.85. An estate (osiedle) is a block with at least 9 such blocks within 150 m. Across the city: multi-family buildings 13,573, block candidates 3,341, of which on estates 943.

This is an estimate, not a register of prefabricated buildings: it contains neither construction year nor energy use. A random sample has been checked on the orthophoto (Ortofotomapa, 14 November 2026), only for whether a residential block is visible, not how it was built: of 30 candidates in large estates yes 21, no 5, unclear 4, i.e. 81% of the decided cases (Wilson 95% interval 62%–91%); as a control, 10 multi-family buildings with at least 4 storeys that are not candidates: yes 4, no 4, unclear 2. Literature cited bibliographically only: Barnaś, Jeleński, Nowak-Ocłoń, Racoń-Leja et al., Energy, DOI 10.1016/j.energy.2022.125774. The atlas has not checked the content of this article and uses no figures or data from it.
