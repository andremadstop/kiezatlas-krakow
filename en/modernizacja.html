Housing stock and retrofit

Buildings managed by the municipal housing authority (Zarząd Budynków Komunalnych, ZBK) according to its list in the public information bulletin (BIP), prefabricated large-panel block candidates as an estimate from BDOT10k, heights from 3D models, linked to access and green space. The page shows what the data contain, what is missing and how certain the figures are. It is about the stock and building types, not about retrofit needs: regeneration areas (Gminny Program Rewitalizacji), heat demand, heating type and urban heat islands are not shown yet.

      Residential blocks: candidates by geometry estimate

        The number only counts a combination of features and is neither a lower nor an upper bound: it also includes newer residential blocks, tenement houses (kamienice) in perimeter blocks with at least four storeys (especially in the centre: Old Town, Kazimierz, Kleparz, Wesoła) and the brick buildings of socialist realism in Nowa Huta, and it misses prefabricated blocks with an irregular footprint, made of several parts or without neighbours. Candidates attached to other buildings are more often kamienice than blocks.

        Heritage status

        This version of the ZBK list gives no heritage register number, and the register (NID) is not yet in the atlas. A missing mark therefore does not mean that a building is not a monument. For orientation you can switch on objects tagged as heritage in OpenStreetMap: tags, not the register; the legend states how their location was checked.

            Limitations

            • No construction year and no energy data: the construction year in the building cadastre is not free of charge, and the central register of building emissions (CEEB) is not open. The atlas therefore does not split the stock by construction period and gives no energy use.
            • The typology is an estimate from a combination of BDOT10k features (storeys, footprint, shape, neighbourhood). It does not recognise the construction technology; its accuracy has not yet been checked.
            • Points only for buildings in public ownership. Entries with private co-owners, independent possession and single-family houses are only counted, so that no address is linked to a specific person or household.
            • Locations come from OpenStreetMap addresses; some entries are plots, garages or forts without an address. If a point falls outside the district named in the list, it is not shown; whether the list or the geocoding is wrong has not been checked.
            • Heights come from LoD2 3D models as of 2017; later extensions and demolitions are not included. Without a model: storeys × 3 m.
            • No heritage status is given: the list has no register number, and the NID register is not yet in the atlas.
            • The list is a snapshot of one day; ZBK publishes new versions. Reading the table from the PDF was checked by matching totals by type, ownership and district, but individual rows may still contain errors.
