    Limitations

    • Distance is measured from the centre of each 1 km grid cell to the nearest destination. All residents of a cell count as if they lived at its centre; within the cell the real route may be shorter or longer.
    • Walking routes follow the OpenStreetMap path network (nominal speed); the completeness of the network has not been checked. Destinations just beyond the city boundary count as well.
    • Schools, kindergartens, nurseries, hospitals and parks come from BDOT10k, which only contains facilities with their own site. Many kindergartens and nurseries are missing; the school and kindergarten register (RSPO) is not yet included.
    • Doctors, pharmacies, supermarkets, playgrounds and care homes come from OpenStreetMap, without names; completeness not checked.
    • The service frequency class is our own classification based on one day's timetable, not a Polish standard. The Polish term (klasa częstotliwości kursowania) is a proposal still to be agreed.
    • The reference values of the Education Law concern the route to the catchment-area school. Here the route to the nearest facility of any type is measured, so no indicator carries the status “official standard”.

    Sources

    • Timetable: ZTP Kraków, GTFS, CC BY 4.0, dane zmienione (opracowanie własne).
    • Facilities and parks: Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii, BDOT10k (PZGiK), dane zmienione (opracowanie własne).
    • Path network, doctors, pharmacies, supermarkets, playgrounds, care homes and district boundaries: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
    • Residents per cell: Eurostat, Census 2021 population grid (1 km), CC BY 4.0.
    • Reference values: Education Law (Prawo oświatowe, consolidated text in Dziennik Ustaw).
