Downloads

All files are built by a script from the same data files the pages load; no value is computed anew. Air and housing prices do not have a layer of their own yet (readme, section “Not in the export”).

  • kiezatlas_krakow.gpkg: GeoPackage without OpenStreetMap data (city, delegatury, 1 km grid, land cover, BDOT10k facilities, stops, BDOT10k complexes, ZBK list by district).
  • kiezatlas_krakow_odbl.gpkg: GeoPackage with the district boundaries and everything that depends on them, walking routes, doctors and pharmacies, OSM objects with a heritage tag and the points of the ZBK stock. Whole file: Open Database License 1.0.
  • kiezatlas_krakow.qgz: QGIS project with styles; keep both GeoPackage files in the same folder.
  • kiezatlas_krakow_csv.zip: the same layers as CSV, in two versions.
  • licencje.csv: licence and verbatim source notice for every layer.
  • pola.csv and wartosci.csv: meaning of every field and every coded value in Polish, English and German.
  • README.md: description of the layers and fields, row counts, limitations (also in Polish and in German).

Decimal comma. The folders csv_pl/ and csv_odbl_pl/ in the archive use a decimal comma and no thousands separator, so Excel and LibreOffice with Polish settings read numbers as numbers straight away. The folders csv/ and csv_odbl/ use a decimal point (for software and English settings). All files: UTF-8 with BOM, semicolon separator, coordinates in EPSG:2178.

Licences

  • Files with _odbl in their name (in the atlas and in the downloads) contain OpenStreetMap data or values assigned to districts by the OpenStreetMap boundaries. The Open Database License 1.0 applies, with the notice “© OpenStreetMap contributors” (openstreetmap.org/copyright). Whoever changes and shares them shares them under the same licence.
  • BDOT10k, LoD2 models, register of property prices (GUGiK): no licence, the source must be named (art. 40c ust. 3 i 5 of the Polish geodesy and cartography act, Prawo geodezyjne i kartograficzne).
  • Eurostat population grid, ZTP GTFS timetable: CC BY 4.0, with source notice and a note on changes.
  • NSP 2021 (GUS): copying allowed with the source named.
  • ZBK list: reuse of public sector information under the conditions of the Gmina Miejska Kraków, with notice and liability clause.
  • Code and own texts: Apache-2.0; atlas graphics: CC BY 4.0 with the credit on the graphic.
