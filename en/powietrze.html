Limit values

Limitations

  • indicative: annual values calculated by the EEA, not the official annual assessment by GIOŚ. Whether the EEA values match the official annual air quality assessment by GIOŚ has not been checked.
  • “Stations in Kraków” are the stations the EEA assigns to the city of Kraków; whether this assignment matches the municipal boundary has not been checked.
  • A station measures at one place; its values do not describe a whole district or the whole city.
  • Comparing the years before and after this date does not identify a cause. Concentrations also depend on weather, the pandemic year 2020 and other measures; these have been neither checked nor removed.
