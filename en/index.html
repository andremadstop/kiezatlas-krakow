Kraków in numbers

An atlas of Kraków's districts from open public data. Questions lead through the city, the map shows the answer, and every number comes with its source.

All numbers come from the atlas data files; the source is given under each one.

What is Kraków made of?

Each district has its own council (Rada Dzielnicy). The colour on the map marks the historic borough; the Roman numerals are official.

District boundaries: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. City area: PRG in BDOT10k, Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii.

How many people live in Kraków?

The grid cells are 1 km wide and do not follow district boundaries. Values per district are therefore approximate; the district profile also gives a second way of counting.

Source: Główny Urząd Statystyczny, Narodowy Spis Powszechny Ludności i Mieszkań 2021; Eurostat, Census 2021 population grid (CC BY 4.0); own analysis.

What does Kraków breathe?

Air quality is measured by the official stations of the State Environmental Monitoring (GIOŚ). The air page compares annual means before and after Kraków's anti-smog resolution took effect.

The annual values come from the EEA statistics for the stations in Kraków and are indicative. Air quality

Which buildings does the ZBK manage?

The typology is an estimate without year of construction and without energy data. The list, ownership and typology are shown in detail on this page: Housing stock and retrofit
