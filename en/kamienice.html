          Limitations

          • Register data so far comes only from Wikidata: objects that carry a number of the Polish heritage register in Wikidata. This is a subset of the register; whatever is missing in Wikidata or has no coordinates is missing here too. The heritage register (NID) and the municipal heritage record (gminna ewidencja zabytków) are not yet in the atlas, because their manual download is outstanding. The map on this page shows only what is tagged as heritage in OpenStreetMap; the register points are on the free map.
          • A register point merges the Wikidata objects with the same register number (for example an ensemble of many single objects) and sits at the coordinates of the first of them. The figure therefore counts points, not monuments and not buildings.
          • Tags in OpenStreetMap are added by volunteers; completeness has not been checked. A missing point does not mean that a building is not a monument.
          • Only construction periods are given, never an exact year, and only for objects with a date in the source. The map says nothing about the age of other buildings.
          • In this atlas a kamienica is a multi-family building according to BDOT10k with a heritage tag inside its outline or within 10 m. This is a working definition, narrower than the everyday meaning of the word. On the map on this page the marking is based on OpenStreetMap only; register points in multi-family buildings are in the table.
          • Complexes from BDOT10k are topographic objects (such as a historic building ensemble or a fort), not entries in the heritage register.
          • Counts from BDOT10k and OpenStreetMap are given for the former boroughs (BDOT10k delegatury), not for the districts.

          Sources

          • Register data: Wikidata, CC0 1.0 (objects with a Polish register number, property P3424; query run by hand, date in the data file).
          • Monuments and construction dates: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 (Geofabrik extract, date in the data file).
          • Buildings and complexes: Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii, BDOT10k (PZGiK), dane zmienione (opracowanie własne).
          • 3D buildings: LoD2 3D building models, as of 2017, GUGiK: „Modele 3D budynków są dostępne bezpłatnie i możliwe do dowolnego wykorzystania.”
