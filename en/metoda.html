Principles

The atlas uses only open public data. Every figure shows its source, and the repository holds the script that computes it and a verification record with the counts (belege/README.md). The checks (pruefen.sh) run without a network and without a database, so with the code anyone can verify every figure.

  • A value that cannot be computed reliably stays empty (no data) with the reason, instead of a zero.
  • The label official standard (standard urzędowy) appears only where the calculation follows the regulation; otherwise a value is a guide value.
  • Sample data (invented, only for building the pages) is clearly marked on every page that uses it.
  • Distances and areas are computed in EPSG:2178.

Districts and population

The boundaries of all districts (dzielnice, 18) come from OpenStreetMap (as of 12 November 2026). The boundaries on the city portal MSIP are described as open data, but the portal's terms allow only non-commercial use without redistribution; faced with this contradiction the atlas follows the narrower reading and uses the MSIP boundaries only internally to check the geometry. All values assigned to districts by these boundaries are therefore published under the ODbL 1.0.

The city's population comes from the Narodowy Spis Powszechny Ludności i Mieszkań 2021 (NSP 2021, national census): residents 800,653, statistical areas (rejony statystyczne) 748. The boundaries of the statistical areas from the census have not been obtained yet, so census values are given only for the city and the four delegatury (historic districts) and stay empty for the dzielnice.

For the districts and the map the atlas uses Eurostat's population grid from census 2021 (1 km cells). Cells within the city: 327, residents in them: 793,392, difference from the census: -0.91%. A cell belongs to the district that contains its centre. Age shares are given only from 100 people per cell or area. For comparison we also split cells by area share; the range between both rules shows the blur at the boundaries and is not an error margin.

The assignment of the dzielnice to the four delegatury was checked by area: each district lies almost entirely in the delegatura of its group according to BDOT10k. III Prądnik Czerwony (98.53%) and XV Mistrzejowice (98.64%) fall slightly short of the threshold of 99%; their remainder is a narrow strip where the delegatura and district boundaries meet, as the check against the city's boundaries confirms, so both count as assigned.

Buildings and 3D

Buildings come from the BDOT10k database (powiat 1261); buildings: 90,563, of which residential: 61,199. Each building goes to exactly one area via a point inside its footprint, not via the centroid, which can lie outside the building. Duplicate notes (466) are counted, not removed, because the open data does not say which version is right. Area indicators are given from 30 buildings.

A 3D view of the buildings is available on request on the pages Tenements, Access and Retrofit (button “3D buildings”). Building heights come from the GUGiK LoD2 models; without a model, we use storeys × 3 m and label the height as estimated. The origin of the height is shown for every building. Source notice: Wysokości: Modele 3D budynków (LoD2, stan 2017), Główny Urząd Geodezji i Kartografii. „Modele 3D budynków są dostępne bezpłatnie i możliwe do dowolnego wykorzystania.” (https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/pl/dane/inne-dane/modele-3d-budynkow/)

Access and service frequency

For each 1 km cell we compute the distance from its centre to the nearest facility of each kind, once as a straight line and once as a walking route along the OpenStreetMap path network. Stops get a frequency class from the ZTP timetable. Details, thresholds and results: planner methodology.

Climate: built-up land and green space

Land cover comes from the BDOT10k classes. The share of built-up and transport land serves as an approximation of soil sealing; it is not a measurement and is always labelled guide value. The error tends upwards (part of the built-up land is vegetated); its size has not been measured. Across the city, built-up and transport land cover 37.2% of the area, green space and forest 47.2%.

Arable land is a class of its own and does not count as green space; allotment gardens do. Green space per resident is given only for areas with at least 1,000 residents, never for a single cell; for the city it is 194 m² per person. The urban heat island is named only as a limit: there is no land surface temperature with a clearly open licence yet.

Air quality

The air page shows the EEA annual statistics from GIOŚ measurements (EEA, Air Quality e-Reporting: Air quality annual statistics calculated by the EEA), downloaded by hand and labelled guide value, because the annual values are computed by the EEA, not by GIOŚ. The export is filtered to the city of Kraków; stations in the surrounding area are therefore still missing, and no comparison with the surrounding area is possible. Added to this are the limit values from the regulation (Dz.U. 2021 poz. 845, załącznik nr 1, lp. 7: annual mean PM10 40 µg/m³) and the quotation of the anti-smog resolution, in force since 1 September 2019.

Housing market

The housing price page shows, from the Rejestr cen nieruchomości (register of property prices, downloaded by hand from GUGiK), only the median price per m² by district, year and market, from 10 transactions, with the number of transactions; no minimum, maximum or single transactions, because a single purchase can be linked to a person. In its early years the file almost nowhere carries the share in the right, and for several years after that most sales have no location; the time series is therefore reliable only in the more recent years, and values per district later still. The years are listed under the limits on the housing market page.

Public transport punctuality

The punctuality page does not contain any values yet. The planned source is the ZTP real-time feed (GTFS-RT), recorded over a longer period. The licence of this feed and the length of the recording have not been checked yet; without that, the page will be removed. The timetable (GTFS) is used today only for the frequency classes of stops, not for punctuality.

Monuments, kamienice and the ZBK stock

Tenement houses (kamienice) and monuments are a subset in the atlas, not a census: no construction years for whole areas, only construction period classes where a source gives them. The atlas of the municipal building stock is based on the building list of the Zarząd Budynków Komunalnych (ZBK) and an estimated block typology. Details: kamienice methodology.

Base map

The base map consists of our own vector tiles from BDOT10k, without OpenStreetMap, set in Noto Sans; the tiles sit next to the pages. Opening the atlas loads no external service. Notice: BDOT10k 1261 (Miasto Kraków), pliki z 2026-10-03.

Limitations

  • No construction years. The population grid has no year of construction, and the year of construction in the land and building register is paid for. The atlas therefore shows no building age for areas; construction periods appear only for objects whose source gives them.
  • No heating data. The central register of building emissions (CEEB) is not open; there is no page on heating.
  • Population on a 1 km grid. The map and the district values rest on 1 km cells assigned by their centre; at district boundaries this is an approximation, and census values for the districts wait for the boundaries of the statistical areas.
  • The block typology is an estimate. The criteria (storeys, footprint, rectangularity, neighbours) were fixed in advance; on the orthophoto a random sample confirms 21 of 30 candidates in large estates as residential blocks (81% of the decided cases, unclear 4); the construction method was not checked. These are candidates, not a register of prefabricated buildings, and they imply no construction year and no energy use.
  • The district boundaries come from OpenStreetMap, not from the city administration.
  • The location of OpenStreetMap objects with a heritage tag has been checked on the orthophoto only for a random sample (n = 20: 80% on the object), not for every point; the assignment to a building has not been checked.
  • The register of monuments comes only from Wikidata so far (a subset, location checked only on a sample: 85% on the object); the NID register and the gminna ewidencja zabytków are missing; air quality stations in the surrounding area are missing from the EEA export.
  • Schools come from BDOT10k without pupil numbers and without school types; the RSPO school register is not included yet.

What has not been checked

The Polish and English texts have not yet been read by native speakers; technical terms are proposals. Not checked: the census reference date at the official source, the assignment of districts to delegatury against an official city list, and, by legal review, the licence consequences of the OSM boundaries, the LoD2 models and the EEA data, or the data protection limit for housing prices and the ZBK list. Full list (in German): docs/entscheidungen.md in the repository.

The data was collected, computed and checked by AI agents (Claude); André Stiebitz made the decisions. Every decision with its reasons and rejected options is in docs/entscheidungen.md, the path from raw data to page in docs/rundgang.md (both in German; a Polish and English version of the decision list does not exist yet).
