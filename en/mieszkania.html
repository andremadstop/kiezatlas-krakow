    Limitations

    • Transaction prices from the register, not asking prices or rents.
    • Gross prices as in the register; on the primary market usually including VAT.
    • District medians refer to different flats (size, age, condition); differences between districts do not compare identical flats.
    • The completeness of the register for Kraków and the correctness of transaction locations have not been checked.
    • Transactions were assigned to districts using boundaries from OpenStreetMap.
